# Fitchi — notes for Claude

Gamified fitness Tamagotchi: Apple Watch app + iPhone companion. See README.md for layout and roadmap.

## Commands
- `./scripts/bootstrap.sh` regenerates `Fitchi.xcodeproj` from `project.yml`. Run it after adding/removing files. Never edit the .xcodeproj directly.
- `./scripts/test.sh --packages` runs the fast package tests (`swift test`, no simulator).
- `./scripts/test.sh` runs the package tests and builds both apps for the simulator.
- `./scripts/deploy.sh phone --no-launch` installs on your iPhone (the watch app rides along). `--device NAME|UDID` picks a device (`--devices` lists them), `--release` drops the developer tools: friends' phones get `--release` (free Apple ID: reinstall every 7 days, see `docs/FRIENDS.md`).
- `swiftlint lint --strict` must pass (CI enforces it). Formatting is owned by swift-format (`.swift-format`).

## Architecture rules
- **All game rules live in `Packages/FitchiCore`.** It must not import SwiftUI, HealthKit, SwiftData, UIKit or WatchKit. Anything time-dependent takes a `GameClock` (tests use `FixedClock`).
- **Pet stats are computed from timestamps** (`now − lastFedAt`), never decremented by timers or background tasks.
- **Points balance = Σ earned − Σ spent.** Earnings are derived from HealthKit samples, keyed by sample UUID (idempotent across watch and phone), and computed through a versioned `PointsRules`. Spending is an append-only ledger. Skip samples flagged `HKMetadataKeyWasUserEntered`.
- **Content is data:** shop items, pets and achievements come from JSON in `Content/`, not hard-coded Swift.
- **HealthKit** is imported only in `FitchiHealth`. **SwiftData** only in `FitchiData` and app targets.
- **Play on both devices:** the watch and the iPhone have the same core game (care, gifts, quests, shop, collection). `BackupLink` (WatchConnectivity, in FitchiData) sends a `GameSnapshot` both ways after every change and restores a reinstalled watch. Merges must stay idempotent/commutative: newest pet save wins but each need comes from whichever copy changed it last (`Pet.mergingNeeds`, so care on both devices before a sync is all kept), hatched egg wins, ledger union (same id on both: earliest wins, `GameRepository.adopt`), inventory newest + owned union, earliest install date. `BackupLink.watch` (`WatchPresence`) tells the iPhone whether a watch is paired / has the app; `PetHome` (phone env `\.petHome`) words things "Send to watch" vs "Make active". The phone loads with `grantsStarterEgg: false` and calls `grantStarterEggIfNew()` only once it knows no watch is paired (iPhone-only players). WatchConnectivity callbacks run off the main actor: never pass a closure written in a `@MainActor` method as a WC handler.
- **Widgets & reminders:** both apps write a `PetGlance` (incl. the backdrop) to the App Group `group.com.nicholasferreira.fitchi` (`GlanceStore`) after every change; `Apps/Widgets` builds the watch complications (`FitchiWatchWidgets`) and the iPhone Lock/Home Screen widgets (`FitchiPhoneWidgets`, small/medium draw the pet in its lit scene), computing stats from it like the app. Care reminders are local notifications planned by `CareNudges` (never at night, grouped, re-planned on every change), sent by the pet's home only: the watch, or the iPhone when no watch has the app (`PetCompanions.refresh(_:sendsReminders:)`, in `Apps/Shared`, compiled into both apps). `CareReminders.holdsPermissionPrompt` keeps the permission prompt off a hatch reveal.
- **SwiftData models must be CloudKit-compatible:** every property optional or defaulted, no `@Attribute(.unique)`, optional relationships. Dedupe in repositories.
- Swift 6 strict concurrency; `@Observable` view models; Swift Testing (`import Testing`) for tests.
- Randomness (hatching, trait rolls) takes `inout some RandomNumberGenerator`; tests use `SeededRandom`.
- Tests load the real `Content/` JSON, and `Catalog.validate()` must return no problems.

## Game design (tunable numbers live in `DecayConfig.v1`, `PointsRules.current`, `HatchRules.v1`, `ComebackQuest.v1`, `VariantRules.v1`, `DuplicateRules.v1`, `MomentRules.v1`, `QuestRules.v1`)
- Currency is **Pep**. "Treat" is a shop item, not the currency.
- Stats: hunger, happiness, energy, cleanliness, 0–100 where 100 = satisfied. Decay slows at night (22:00–07:00). Resting (non-active) pets decay at 25%.
- Pets never die. Neglect escalates sad → sick → packing → ranAway (after ~6 days of neglect, typically more than a week from full stats). A ran-away pet returns after a comeback quest (60 active minutes). Vacations pause both decay and the neglect clock.
- Pep = minutes × 1.2 × activity multiplier (run > walk) × effort bonus. Effort comes from average heart rate vs max HR (`EffortEstimator`). Overlapping samples count once (`ActivitySample.resolvingOverlaps`: workouts beat passive exercise minutes). Rounding is applied to each day's running total, never per sample. Hand-entered and pre-install activity earns nothing. Welcome bonus 100. Current rules are `PointsRules.v3`: real workouts always earn in full (up to 6 h each); the daily soft cap of 180 (half rate beyond, also the daily Pep goal) only applies to passive exercise minutes. Everyday steps earn 1 Pep per 200 outside workouts/exercise minutes, max 25/day; on **steps-only days** (no exercise-minute samples, i.e. no watch worn, e.g. iPhone-only players) 1 per 100 with no cap. One step earning per day keyed `steps:YYYY-MM-DD`; Health returns hourly totals merged across devices.
- Duplicates: an egg's rarity roll and the 1/64 shiny chance are never changed. Within the rolled rarity, unfound colourings get 4× weight, and after 2 repeats in a row the next hatch is guaranteed new if that rarity has anything left (`HatchHistory`, replayed from the roster). An exact repeat refunds 25% of the egg price as a ledger adjustment with id `repeat-refund:<petID>`. Repeats are kept as their own pets; nobody gets released.
- Special hatches: `Weather`, `Season`, `TimeOfDay` live in Core `World/` (pure functions of the date; FitchiUI only draws them). A colouring with `"when": {weather|time|season}` in `species.json` only hatches when the `HatchMoment` (season, settled weather, time of day: dawn 5–8, day, dusk 18–21, night 21–5) matches; each species has two (one weather, one time/season). `"affinities": [{when, boost}]` scale a species' share *within* the rolled rarity (only matters where a rarity has several species). Rarity odds, the 1/64 shiny and duplicate protection are unchanged. Core defaults to `HatchMoment.plain` (nothing special) so tests are deterministic; `PetStore.hatchMoment` passes the real one. The shop shows a `HatchMomentBanner`; the collection badges specials with their condition emoji.
- Pets come from eggs with rarity odds. The free starter is a random **common** species (dog, cat, axolotl). Each pet rolls its own traits within its species' range, plus a personality and a colour variant (weighted; 1/64 shiny). Variant colours are `#RRGGBB` data in `species.json`.
- Pets are vector art drawn in FitchiUI `PetArt` (one drawing per species, recoloured by palette; `PetPose` drives animation). Preview: `cd Packages/FitchiUI && swift run fitchi-art-preview OUT.png`. Backdrops are independent of pets. Behaviour (wandering, catnaps, night sleep, mood faces/effects, care animations) is `PetLife.frame(at:seed:mood:reaction:)`: a pure function of time like the stats, no stored state; `PetMood` (Core) says what the pet visibly needs.
- Fonts: Baloo 2 (`FitchiTheme.Fonts.display`) for names, numbers, buttons; SF Pro Rounded (`.text`) for running text.
- Art styles are purchasable items (`category: artStyle`). Core only knows style ids; rendering picks the art.
- Backdrops are shop items (`category: backdrop`, ids `backdrop-*`; the meadow is the free default, `Catalog.defaultBackdropID`). The selection lives in `Inventory.activeBackdropID` and syncs with the backup. Permanent ownership only grows: merges union `ownedItemIDs`, and ledger purchases of non-consumables always count as owned.
- The phone shop sells eggs, backdrops and accessories; consumables are bought by tapping care (`PhonePetView` on the iPhone home, `PetView` on the watch; both use FitchiUI `CareEvent` for the reaction). Don't sell art styles until they render ("Coming soon").
- Accessories (`category: accessory`, one per `AccessorySlot`) live in `Pet.equipped`, so they save and sync with the pet. Drawings are in FitchiUI `AccessoryArt`, placed per species by `PetFit`; a test fails if an accessory on sale has no drawing. Dress pets with `PetStore.wear`/`takeOff` (phone shop or the watch's Wardrobe on the pet page). Preview: `swift run fitchi-art-preview --accessories OUT.png`.
- Daily engagement (Core `Engagement/`): **pet moments** are 3 gifts a day (windows 7–10, 11:30–14:30, 17–20:30; each open 2 h at a time seeded by the date, 5–12 Pep, 10% lucky 25). Collected on either device (gift bubble on the pet screen), announced by notifications that share the care-reminder switch, hinted on the complication. **Daily quests**: 3 a day (early movement, movement, care/gifts) picked by date, progress computed from steps, samples and the ledger (care = a purchase of that category today). Finishing all adds a streak day (one missed day forgiven, two end it) and pays that day's rung of `StreakLadder.v1`: 15/20/25/35/45/60 Pep, then a free Sparkle Egg on day 7 (Golden every 4th week). Egg rewards are 0-Pep `egg-token:<day>:<eggID>` ledger entries, hatched on the phone only (`hatchRewardEgg`, marked `egg-opened:<token>`). All rewards are ledger `adjustment`s with fixed ids (`moment:<day>:<n>`, `quest:<day>:<goal>`, `quests-done:<day>`, `egg-token:<day>:<egg>`), so they sync and pay once; both apps call `claimQuests` before `claimAchievements`.
- Achievements are data (`Content/achievements.json`: criterion + reward + SF Symbol `icon`). `PetStore.claimAchievements` pays rewards as ledger entries with a fixed id per achievement (so they sync and never pay twice) and queues `celebrations` for the UI. Both apps claim after activity refreshes and game changes.
- Backdrops: 9 scenes in `SceneBackdrop` (outdoor in `OutdoorBackdrops.swift`, home scenes in `IndoorBackdrops.swift`); ground/floor sits at y ≈ 50–56 of the 100-wide art space. `PetStage` finds the ground from the stage *width* (backdrops scale to width), never a fraction of the height: feet go between `width × 0.6` (the back, just below the horizon) and the front of the ground (above the watch controls, or near the bottom with `controlsBelow: false` on the phone). `PetFrame.depth` (0 back … 1 front) comes from `PetLife` strolls; pets at the back are drawn up to 22% smaller.
- Living scenes: `SceneBackdrop(kind, time:, light:)` animates (clouds, grass, ripples, snow, waves, steam) from a clock and is lit by `SceneLight` (dawn/dusk glow, sun arc, night with moon, stars, fireflies and lamps), computed from the local time. `time: 0` and `.day` give the still shop picture. Outdoor scenes get **visitors** (butterfly, bird in snow, firefly at night) that the pet turns to watch (`PetLife.visit`, about 1 resting slot in 8). The pet says hello after 2 min away and runs to the front for a big hello after 4 h (`PetLife.Greeting`, last seen in `PetStage.lastSeenKey`). Preview: `swift run fitchi-art-preview --scenes OUT.png`.
- Seasons (`Season`, from the month; flipped for southern-hemisphere regions) recolour the meadow, pond and forest via `SceneWorld.greenery` and add petals/leaves/frost and snow (pond freezes, birds instead of butterflies, no fireflies); the snowy hill and beach never change. Outdoor scenes get a horizon `Haze`; the pet is tinted to the scene light (`SceneLight.petTint`) and slightly desaturated at the back. Strolls leave a `PetLife.trail` (derived from time, like everything else): footprints in snow/sand, dust puffs on grass, nothing indoors (`Kind.footing`). While standing about, a pet now and then does its personality's habit (`PetLife+Habits`, ~1 resting slot in 3, never alongside a visitor).
- Weather (`Weather`, FitchiUI): a pure function of date + season, not real-world weather. Each day rolls clear/cloudy/rain/wind/snow (`Weather.odds`, snow only in winter); rain, wind and snow come as one 2–6 h spell between 7:00 and midnight with 30 min ramps, grey skies around a shower. `adapted(to:)` per scene (starry night always clear, snowy hill snows, beach never snows). Drawn by `WeatherArt` over outdoor scenes (grey cloud bank, rain streaks, puddle rings, gusts; sway via `SceneWorld.sway`) and through indoor windows. The pet reacts via `Cues.weather` (`PetLife+Weather`: shakes off rain, puddle hops with `effects.splash`, ears blown back, catches snowflakes); indoors it stays dry.
- Live cheers: `Cheers.reached` (Core, `CheerRules.v1`: 5k/10k/15k/20k steps, 30/60/90 active minutes, each real workout ≥10 min, the daily Pep goal) computes today's `Milestone`s with stable ids from Health data. Each device keeps its own `CheerLog` (UserDefaults) of what it has cheered; `CheerBoard` plays the most important unplayed one after each activity refresh (pet jumps with confetti via `Cues.cheer`, `.cheer` sound, headline, success haptic). The watch posts a notification for milestones reached in the background (`ActivityStore.onRefresh` → `CareReminders.announceCheers`, shares the care-reminder switch, never 22–7).
- Touch and afterglow ride in `PetLife.Cues`: a tap on the pet is a **boop** (4 reactions in turn, light haptic), holding 0.35 s **pets** it (eyes closed, hearts), and a sleeping pet only stirs. After a real workout (≥10 min, not passive, not hand-entered) the pet is **pumped** for an hour (`Afterglow`/`AfterglowRules.v1` in Core): bouncier, with hops and sparkles, and the headline says so. The Digital Crown is left alone: it pages the watch app.
- Sounds are synthesized in code (FitchiUI `Sound/`): no audio files. The species sets an `Accent` (cats mew, dogs woof, bunnies squeak, axolotls gurgle, red pandas twitter, penguins honk); each pet's `PetVoice` (pitch within its species range, tone incl. a rare robot, brightness, vibrato, pace, a quirk) is rolled from a hash of its whole id (single UUID bytes are fixed), so it never needs saving. `PetSound` phrases (boop ×4, purr, stir, hello, big hello, care, happy) render through `PetSynth` (pure, tested) and play via `PetSounds.shared` as ambient audio (follows the iPhone silent switch; `PetSounds.enabledKey` switch on both apps). `StageSounds` hooks them to the stage; never while the wrist is down. Listen: `swift run fitchi-art-preview --sounds DIR`.
- Phone **Pets tab** (`PetsView`, `PetTiles`, `PetFilterSheet`, `PetPage`): every pet owned, with search, quick-filter chips, a filter sheet, sort, grid/list and group-by-species (view prefs in `@AppStorage`). The rules are Core `RosterQuery` (filter/sort/group, favourites first), `RosterStats` and `ColouringShelf` (a species' colourings with the pets you own in each). Favourites are `Pet.isFavorite` (stored as optional `favorite` so old saves load), set with `PetStore.setFavorite`, and sync with the pet like its name. Home shows a short strip with "See all". **Compare** 2–3 pets side by side (`PetCompareView`; pick them in the Pets tab's Compare mode or via "Compare with…" on a pet's page): Core `PetComparison` gives trait rows (highest stands out) and need rows (decay multiplier from traits × personality; lowest = easiest stands out) plus notes. Grids are alive: `LivingPetView` (idle + a personality habit or hop every 11–15 s, `framesPerSecond` 12 in grids, still with Reduce Motion) is used for Pets tiles, found Collection cards and the Home strip; silhouettes stay still.
- `CollectionBook` (Core) is the Pokédex: every species × colouring + shiny, found from the roster (pets never leave it). Undiscovered entries render as solid silhouettes.
- Bundle IDs: `com.nicholasferreira.fitchi` (phone), `.watchkitapp` (watch), `.watchkitapp.widgets` (complications).
